# TalkMe A2–C2 curriculum

Source: Muhammad G‘apporov and Robiya Qosimova, *Ingliz tili grammatikasi*, Turon-Iqbol, 2010. The page ranges refer to the supplied textbook. The situations, level assignments, exercises and English titles are authored for TalkMe. The textbook does not assign CEFR levels.

A2 follows the existing A0 and A1 lessons. Each unit uses **Qoida → Misollar → Mashqlar → Wrap-up**. The rule starts in Uzbek with a concrete situation, the examples use original English and Uzbek translations with TTS, and the exercises provide immediate feedback. The Wrap-up restates one usable pattern, gives a small real-life challenge, and offers specific encouragement. Later levels increase the complexity of the communicative task while keeping the Uzbek explanation accessible.

## Implementation status

This roadmap defines 300 lesson slots. The app seed implements all 60 lessons each for A2, B1 and B2, plus four sample lessons each for C1 and C2. The remaining 112 lesson slots still need full lesson copy, bilingual examples and interactive exercises. The coverage table tracks chapters. A subsection-by-subsection audit is still pending.

### Example lesson — Ordering and Shopping

**Qoida.** Kafeda ikki sendvich va suv olmoqchisiz. Sanab bo‘ladigan narsaga *How many?* deysiz: *How many sandwiches?* Suvning miqdorini yoki buyurtma narxini *How much?* bilan so‘raysiz.

**Misollar.** “Could I have a bottle of water, please?” — “Iltimos, bir shisha suv bersangiz.” “How many tickets do you need?” — “Sizga nechta chipta kerak?” “How much is it altogether?” — “Hammasi bo‘lib qancha turadi?”

**Mashqlar.** Choose the missing phrase in “___ milk do we need?”, select the polite way to order two sandwiches, then listen to a short order and identify which item the customer wants two of. Show an Uzbek explanation after the correct answer.

**Wrap-up.** Nechta olma? *How many apples?* Qancha suv? *How much water?* Endi kafeda suv va ikkita sendvich so‘rab ko‘ring. Bir qisqa iltimosning o‘zi suhbatni boshlashga yetadi.
